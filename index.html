ABOUT

Watch Oscars Simulator is a decision game where you run an independent watch manufacture and try to win the most prestigious award in watchmaking — the "Oscars of Watches."

Balance your cash reserves against the Founder's happiness. Push too hard for money and he quits. Spend too freely and you go bankrupt.
